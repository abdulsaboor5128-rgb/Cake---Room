About us

A small kitchen with big care

THE CAKE ROOM is a home bakery in Karachi. Every order is baked fresh in small batches, so nothing sits on a shelf waiting for you.

We keep the menu short and the recipes honest: good butter, real chocolate, and decoration that looks beautiful without being fussy.

Freshly Baked

Baked to order, never the day before. Your cake leaves the oven shortly before it reaches you.

Premium Ingredients

Real butter, quality cocoa, fresh cream and seasonal fruit. No shortcuts, no artificial flavours.

Made With Love

Every cake is mixed, baked and decorated by hand, and packed like a gift.
